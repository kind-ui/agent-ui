## Composition

```text
GuardrailNotice
├── GuardrailNoticeIcon
├── GuardrailNoticeTitle
├── GuardrailNoticeReason
├── GuardrailNoticeDetailTrigger
├── GuardrailNoticeDetail
├── GuardrailNoticeActions
└── GuardrailNoticeDismiss
```

`GuardrailNotice` renders these parts. `GuardrailNoticeTitle` shows the `label`, else "Response blocked" or "Response filtered", and `GuardrailNoticeReason` the `reason`. `GuardrailNoticeDetailTrigger` is the **Why?** button that shows and hides `GuardrailNoticeDetail`, both rendered only with a `detail`. The icon, actions and Dismiss work as in Notice. To arrange the parts yourself, render them inside `GuardrailNoticeRoot`.

## Usage

### Blocking

Pass `blocking` when the guardrail stopped the response. The notice is then an alert, and its announcement interrupts what a screen reader is saying. Without it the notice is a note that waits its turn, for content that was only filtered. The variant defaults to `"warning"`; pass `variant="info"` for a quieter filter notice.

### Detail

Pass `detail` to add **Why?**. Pressing it, or Enter or Space on it, shows the detail, and find in page shows it too when it matches hidden text. Pass `defaultOpen` to start with it shown, or `open` with `onOpenChange` to control it; `onOpenChange` gets `"trigger-press"`, `"find-in-page"` or `"imperative"`.

```tsx
<GuardrailNotice
  blocking
  label="Model output withheld"
  reason="The response included personal data."
  detail="Your organization's policy blocks responses that contain email addresses or phone numbers."
/>
```

At full motion the detail slides open; under `prefers-reduced-motion`, or with `motion="reduced"`, it fades in over 120 ms, and with `motion="none"` it shows at once.

### Dismiss and Announcements

**Dismiss**, `focusFallback` and the announcements work as in [Notice](/docs/components/notice): a guardrail notice added after the page loads announces its title once.

### Agent

An agent connected with `createAgent` finds the notice by its title and reads `open`. `expand()` and `collapse()` show and hide the detail, offered while there is one, and `dismiss()` does what **Dismiss** does, offered while `onDismiss` is set. Like any component with a button, it also has UI SDK's generic `press`.
