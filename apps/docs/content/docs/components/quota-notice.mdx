## Composition

```text
QuotaNotice
├── QuotaNoticeIcon
├── QuotaNoticeTitle
├── QuotaNoticeMeter
├── QuotaNoticeReset
├── QuotaNoticeDescription
├── QuotaNoticeActions
│   └── QuotaNoticeUpgrade
└── QuotaNoticeDismiss
```

`QuotaNotice` renders these parts. `QuotaNoticeTitle` shows the `label`, else "You've used 80% of this month's requests". `QuotaNoticeMeter` is UI SDK's Meter, a bar with the amounts beside it ("800 of 1,000 requests"). `QuotaNoticeReset` says when the quota resets, rendered only with a `resetAt`. `QuotaNoticeDescription` holds any `children`, and `QuotaNoticeActions` holds **Upgrade**, then your `actions`. The icon and Dismiss work as in Notice. To arrange the parts yourself, render them inside `QuotaNoticeRoot`.

## Usage

### Used and Limit

Pass `used` and `limit` in the same unit, and `unit` to name it (default "requests"). Pass `period`, like "this month", for the title to read "this month's requests"; without it, it reads "your requests". At the limit the title reads "You've used all of…".

```tsx
<QuotaNotice used={800} limit={1000} period="this month" />
```

### Variant

The variant follows the quota: `"info"` below `warnAt` (default 0.8), `"warning"` from it, and `"error"` at the limit. Pass `variant` to choose it yourself. The bar takes the variant's color, and the mark is named by it, so the level doesn't depend on color alone.

### Reset Time

Pass `resetAt`, an ISO date-time, to show "Resets in 3 days". Under a day it counts down in hours, minutes and seconds. The countdown follows the clock once the notice is in the page; server markup shows the date instead ("Resets on December 1, 2026"), so it hydrates without a mismatch. Pass `now` to count from a fixed moment rather than the clock, as the example does.

### Upgrade and Dismiss

Pass `onUpgrade` to show **Upgrade**, and `upgradeLabel` to change its words. It gets `{ reason, source }`, which says whether a person pressed it (`"mouse"`, `"keyboard"`) or an agent asked (`"agent"`). **Dismiss** and `focusFallback` work as in [Notice](./notice.mdx).

### Announcements

The notice announces its title when the variant changes, so a screen reader hears "You've used 80% of this month's requests" once when it starts to warn and once more at the limit. A new `used` within a variant changes the title and the bar without a word, and the reset countdown is never announced. A notice added after the page loads announces its title once, as Notice does.

### Agent

An agent connected with `createAgent` finds the notice by its title. `upgrade()` does what **Upgrade** does, offered while `onUpgrade` is set, and `dismiss()` does what **Dismiss** does, offered while `onDismiss` is set. The bar isn't an entry of its own. Like any component with a button, it also has UI SDK's generic `press`.
