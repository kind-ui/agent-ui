Use an effort slider in a [composer](./composer.mdx)'s controls when the model can trade speed for more thinking and the row has no room for every level. It's a button that shows the chosen level and opens a popover holding UI SDK's Slider, with one step per level, a Reset and an optional line naming the model.

```

```

## Composition

```text
EffortSlider
├── EffortSliderTrigger
└── EffortSliderContent
    ├── EffortSliderSlider
    │   └── EffortSliderTitle
    ├── EffortSliderStops
    ├── EffortSliderModel
    └── EffortSliderReset
```

`EffortSlider` is a group named by its `label` and the chosen level. `EffortSliderTrigger` is the button that opens the popover and shows the chosen level's label, unless you pass children. `EffortSliderContent` is the popover. `EffortSliderSlider` is UI SDK's Slider, with `EffortSliderTitle` and the chosen level's label above the track. `EffortSliderStops` draws a tick and label under the track for each level. `EffortSliderModel` is the line naming the model, and `EffortSliderReset` returns to the default level. `EffortSlider` renders the trigger and content, and the content its parts, when you pass no children.

## Usage

### Levels

Pass `levels`, from least to most effort, each with the `value` your app receives and the `label` a person reads. Without them, the levels are Low, Medium and High. A flame beside the level grows and warms with effort, on the button and above the track, and the fill warms as it grows. Under the track, the level under the thumb lights up the moment a drag reaches it, before it's reported.

The button is named by the `label` and the chosen level, like "Effort, High". The `label` is "Effort" by default and also titles the popover and names the slider's thumb, whose value text is the level's label.

### Value

Pass `value` and `onValueChange` to control the chosen level, or `defaultValue` to start with one. `onValueChange` gets the level's `value` once a person settles on it: a drag reports once, when it ends, not at every level it passes. The reason is `drag`, `step-key` or `track-press` from UI SDK Slider, `reset-press` from Reset, or `imperative` from `setValue`. Call `details.cancel()` to keep the old level. Pass `disabled` while the model can't change its effort; the button then doesn't open the popover.

```tsx
<EffortSlider
  levels={levels}
  value={effort}
  onValueChange={(next) => setEffort(next)}
/>
```

### Reset

**Reset** returns to `defaultLevel`, which is the middle level unless you name one by its `value`. It's present only while another level is chosen, and it shares a row with the model line, so it comes and goes without moving the slider. When it leaves while focused, focus moves to the thumb.

### Model

Pass `modelLabel` to add a line under the slider, like "Effort for GPT-5", that tells people which model the effort applies to. Add `modelHref` to make it a link, like to the model's settings, or `onModelPress` to make it a button; `onModelPress` gets the `modelLabel` and the reason `model-press`. With both, the line is a link.

### Opening

Pass `open` and `onOpenChange` to control whether the popover is open, or `defaultOpen` to start open. `onOpenChange` gets UI SDK Popover's reason, like `trigger-press` or `escape-key`.

### Keyboard

The button is one Tab stop. Enter or Space opens the popover with focus on the thumb. The arrow keys move one level, and Home and End move to the first and last. Each key reports the level it lands on. Escape closes the popover, with focus back on the button.

### Presentation and Motion

The popover opens below the button. With `presentation="auto"`, the default, it's a bottom sheet on small touch screens instead; pass `"popover"` or `"sheet"` to choose one. `motion` sets how it opens and closes and how the thumb answers: at full motion it squishes as you drag, bumps as it lands on a level and glows once at the last. `"auto"`, the default, follows `prefers-reduced-motion`; `"reduced"` only fades the popover and swaps levels at once, and `"none"` moves nothing.

For an inline row of levels with no popover, use [Reasoning Effort](./reasoning-effort.mdx).

### Agent

An agent connected with `createAgent` finds the effort slider by its name, like "Effort, High", and reads `open` and `value`. It lists every level by `value` and label. `setValue` takes a level and refuses one that isn't listed; it's offered while the slider is enabled, and it changes the level whether the popover is open or closed. `reset` is offered only while another level than the default is chosen. `open` is offered while the popover is closed and enabled, and `close` while it's open. None needs a person to confirm.
