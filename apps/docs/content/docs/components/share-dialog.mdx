## Composition

```text
ShareDialog
├── ShareDialogTrigger
├── ShareDialogBackdrop
└── ShareDialogContent
    ├── ShareDialogTitle
    ├── ShareDialogDescription
    ├── ShareDialogVisibility
    ├── ShareDialogCreate
    ├── ShareDialogLink
    ├── ShareDialogCopy
    ├── ShareDialogError
    ├── ShareDialogRevoke
    └── ShareDialogClose
```

`ShareDialogTrigger` is the **Share** button, named by `label`. `ShareDialogContent` is a modal dialog over `ShareDialogBackdrop`, titled by `dialogLabel` ("Share chat") and described by `ShareDialogDescription`, which says who can open the chat. `ShareDialogVisibility` is a UI SDK Segmented Control with the two choices, and `ShareDialogLink` the link in a read-only UI SDK Input. `ShareDialogCreate`, `ShareDialogCopy` and `ShareDialogRevoke` are **Create link**, **Copy link** and **Stop sharing**, `ShareDialogError` says what went wrong, and `ShareDialogClose` is **Done**. To arrange the parts yourself, pass them as children of `ShareDialog`.

## Usage

### Who Can Open the Chat

A chat starts private. Pass `defaultVisibility`, or `visibility` with `onVisibilityChange`, to set it yourself. Choosing **Anyone with the link** only changes `visibility`: nothing is shared until there's a link.

### Making the Link

The component never builds a URL. While `visibility` is `"link"`, there's no `link` and `onCreateLink` is set, the dialog shows **Create link**. Make the link in `onCreateLink`, on your server, and pass it back as `link`:

```tsx
<ShareDialog
  visibility={visibility}
  onVisibilityChange={setVisibility}
  link={chat.shareUrl}
  onCreateLink={() => shareChat(chat.id)}
  onRevoke={() => unshareChat(chat.id)}
/>
```

Return a promise to keep **Create link** pending until it settles. If it rejects, the dialog shows "Couldn't create a link", tells screen readers, and offers **Create link** again.

Once there's a `link`, the dialog shows it with **Copy link**, which copies it and reads **Copied** for 2 seconds. `onCopy` runs first, and `details.cancel()` keeps it from writing to the clipboard.

### Stop Sharing

**Stop sharing** shows while there's a `link` and `onRevoke` is set. Revoke the link in `onRevoke`, then clear `link`, and usually set `visibility` back to `"private"`, as the example does. A returned promise keeps it pending, and a rejection shows and announces "Couldn't stop sharing".

### Presentation and Motion

With `presentation="auto"`, the default, the dialog is centered on the page, and a bottom sheet on small touch screens. Pass `"dialog"` or `"sheet"` to choose one. Escape, **Done** or a press outside closes it, and focus returns to **Share**. Pass `defaultOpen`, or `open` with `onOpenChange`, to open it yourself.

The dialog rises in at full motion. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, or with `motion="reduced"`, it only fades, over 120 ms, and with `motion="none"` it opens at once.

### Shared View

Someone who opens a share link should read the chat, not continue it. Render the [conversation](./conversation.mdx) with [messages](./message.mdx) that have no callbacks, so there's nothing to edit or regenerate, under a [notice](./notice.mdx) with a button that starts their own copy:

### Agent

An agent connected with `createAgent` finds the dialog by its `label` and reads `open` and `visibility`. `open()` is offered while the dialog is closed, and the others while it's open: `setVisibility` while nothing is pending, `createLink` and `revoke` while their buttons show, and `copyLink` while there's a link.

Making a chat public is a consequential change, so with `agentConfirm`, on by default, every agent action needs a person's go-ahead. Pass `agentConfirm={false}` only when your app asks for it another way.
