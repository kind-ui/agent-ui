## Composition

```text
Task
├── TaskTrigger
│   ├── TaskIcon
│   ├── TaskTitle
│   └── TaskMeta
└── TaskContent
    └── TaskList
        └── TaskItem
            ├── TaskItemIcon
            ├── TaskItemTitle
            └── TaskItemFiles
```

`Task` renders the trigger and content when you pass no children. Its trigger is a native button that shows or hides the list. `TaskTitle` shows the `label`, or "Task" without one, and `TaskMeta` how many steps are complete, like "1 of 3". `TaskList` renders an item for each of the `items` when you pass it no children, and each item shows its status mark, title and files unless you give it children. `TaskItemFiles` renders only while the item has files.

## Usage

### Items

Pass the steps as `items`, each with an `id`, a `status` of `pending`, `running`, `complete` or `error`, a `title` and optional `files`. Update an item's `status` as it runs, and keep its `id`: focus follows the id.

Without a `status` of its own, the task's status is what the items add up to: `error` once one fails, `complete` once all are complete, `running` once one runs or is complete, and `pending` before that. Pass `status` to set it yourself, like `awaiting-input` while the agent waits for an answer.

### Opening and Closing

The task opens and closes like a [Step](./step.mdx): it opens while it runs or waits for input, closes when it completes, and stays open when it fails, until someone opens or closes it. Pass `open` with `onOpenChange` to control it, or `autoOpen="never"` to keep it closed until someone opens it.

### Keyboard

The items are one Tab stop. The arrow keys move between them, and Home and End go to the first and last. When the focused item is removed, focus moves to its neighbor. Screen readers read each item's status, then its title, and the running one as the current step.

### Streaming

A new item fades in once as it arrives, at the end of the list, so nothing above it moves. A status change morphs only the item's mark, so the rows never shift. A removed item leaves at once.

Screen readers hear an item's title and its outcome, like "Add tests for the limits complete", when it completes or fails, and nothing while it runs. Items that end in the same update, and the task itself, are read in one message.

### Motion

Marks morph between statuses, and the list grows open and shut. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, or with `motion="reduced"`, they fade over 120 ms instead and nothing moves or spins, and with `motion="none"` they change at once.
