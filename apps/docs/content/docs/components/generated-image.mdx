Use a generated image when the agent makes a picture and you want to show it arriving in the conversation. It holds a frame of the image's shape the whole time, so the conversation never jumps: grain over a drifting field, then over a blurred preview that sharpens as it progresses, then the image dissolving in, or what went wrong.

## Installation

```bash tab="React"
npx shadcn@latest add @kind-ui/generated-image
```

```bash tab="Web Components"
npx shadcn@latest add @kind-ui/generated-image-web
```

## Composition

```text
GeneratedImage
├── GeneratedImageFrame
│   ├── GeneratedImagePreview
│   ├── GeneratedImageImage
│   ├── GeneratedImageProgress
│   ├── GeneratedImageError
│   ├── GeneratedImageTrigger
│   └── GeneratedImageDownload
├── GeneratedImageBackdrop
└── GeneratedImageDialog
    ├── GeneratedImageFullImage
    ├── GeneratedImageTitle
    ├── GeneratedImageDownload
    └── GeneratedImageClose
```

`GeneratedImage` is a figure named by its `alt`. `GeneratedImageFrame` takes the image's shape and holds the rest: `GeneratedImagePreview` is the blurred `preview`, `GeneratedImageImage` the image at `src`, `GeneratedImageProgress` a UI SDK Progress, read as a quiet label in the corner while the image is on its way, and `GeneratedImageError` what went wrong. `GeneratedImageTrigger` covers the loaded image as **View full size**, and `GeneratedImageDownload` sits in its corner. `GeneratedImageDialog` shows `GeneratedImageFullImage` with the `alt` as its `GeneratedImageTitle`, **Download** and **Close**, over `GeneratedImageBackdrop`. To arrange the parts yourself, pass them as children of `GeneratedImage`.

## Usage

### From Generating to Loaded

Pass `width` and `height`, the image's size in pixels. The frame takes their shape before anything arrives and keeps it in every phase, so nothing around it moves.

Set `status` to `"generating"` while the image is being made. Until there's a preview, the frame is a soft field of the accent color under film grain. Pass `preview`, the URL of a partial image, when your model streams one: it shows heavily blurred and muted. Pass `progress`, from 0 to 100, when you know how far it has come: the grain thins and the preview sharpens toward it, and a label in the corner reads, for example, **Creating image · 42%**. Screen readers get the same value from the progress bar.

Once the image is made, set `status` to `"ready"` and `src` to its URL. The component then reads the image's own `load` and `error`: it shows **Loading image** until the image is in, then dissolves it in over the preview, grain first, as the last of the grain clears. Keep `preview` until then, so the frame never goes blank. The figure is `aria-busy` until the image loads.

```tsx
<GeneratedImage
  alt="A lighthouse at dusk"
  status={image.url ? "ready" : "generating"}
  progress={image.progress}
  preview={image.partialUrl}
  src={image.url}
  width={1024}
  height={768}
/>
```

### Errors

Set `status` to `"error"` when generation fails, with `error` saying why. An image at `src` that fails to load shows an error too. The frame keeps its size and shows a muted image glyph with the `error`, else "The image couldn't be generated." or "The image couldn't be loaded.".

For an image it saw being generated, the component announces "Image ready" when it loads or "Image failed" when it fails, once for each `src`. Images that were ready when they first rendered, like earlier ones in the conversation, stay quiet.

### Full Size and Download

Press the loaded image, or focus it and press Enter or Space, to open it full size in a modal dialog titled by its `alt`. Escape, **Close** or a press outside closes it, and focus returns to the image. Pass `defaultOpen`, or `open` with `onOpenChange`, to open it yourself. The dialog closes if the image goes back to generating or fails.

**Download** shows while `onDownload` is set and the image has loaded: in the frame's corner on hover or focus, and in the dialog. The component never fetches the file: it calls `onDownload`, and your handler saves it, as the example's `save` does. Return a promise to keep Download pending until it settles; if it rejects, the component announces "Download failed".

### Motion

At full motion the field drifts, the grain flickers while the image is on its way; the preview sharpens smoothly, the image dissolves in and settles, and the dialog rises in. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, or with `motion="reduced"`, the field and grain hold still, the preview sharpens in steps, and the image and dialog fade in over 120 ms. With `motion="none"` nothing animates.

### Agent

An agent connected with `createAgent` finds the image by its `alt` and reads `phase` and `open`. `open()` is offered once the image has loaded and while the dialog is closed, `close()` while it's open, and `download()` while `onDownload` is set, the image has loaded and no download is pending. Like any component that opens, it also has UI SDK's generic `press`.
