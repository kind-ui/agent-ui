Use a step group when one turn runs many steps, like tool calls, commands or agents. Its line names the work: the step that's running while it runs, like "Running `npm test`" with "1 of 3", and what was done once it's finished, like "Read `sign-in.ts`, `session.ts` and ran `npm test`". It opens to your own rows.

## Composition

```text
StepGroup
├── StepGroupTrigger
│   ├── StepGroupIcon
│   ├── StepGroupTitle
│   └── StepGroupMeta
└── StepGroupContent
    └── StepGroupChildren
```

`StepGroup` renders these parts and puts its children, your rows, in `StepGroupChildren`. The trigger is a native button named by the title and the meta, like "Read auth.ts and ran npm test, 1 failed, 41 sec". `StepGroupIcon` is the group's status mark. To arrange the parts yourself, render them inside `StepGroupRoot`.

## Usage

### Items and Kinds

The group can't read your rows, so pass what they are as `items`: each step's `id`, `status`, and optionally its `kind` and `target`. Pass `kinds` to say how each kind reads, live and done, with its noun: `{ read: { live: "Reading", done: "Read", one: "file", many: "files" } }`. The title joins kinds in the order they were first used and names up to two targets each, so "Read `sign-in.ts`, `session.ts` and ran `npm test`"; past `maxClauses` kinds (2 by default) it counts instead, like "Read 6 files, edited 2 files and 3 other steps". Without `kinds`, it reads "12 steps". Pass `current` to word the running step yourself, or `label` to replace the title.

Each row says its own status, so a row is a [Tool](./tool.mdx), [Terminal](./terminal.mdx) or [Subagent](./subagent.mdx), titled in words. Wrap them in a [StepList](./step.mdx) to make them one Tab stop. Complete rows are quiet: their mark is a dot, not a check, and only running, waiting, failed and stopped rows are marked.

### Status

The group takes its most urgent step's status: waiting, then failed, then running, then not started, then stopped, then complete. Pass `status` to set it yourself, like `error` when the turn itself failed. The meta says it in words:

| While | Meta |
| --- | --- |
| Running | "3 of 5" |
| Waiting, open | "1 waiting" |
| Waiting, folded | "Waiting for approval" |
| Done | the duration, like "41 sec", from `durationSeconds` |
| Done with a failure | "1 failed · 41 sec" |
| Stopped | "Stopped after 41 sec" |
| Failed | "Failed after 12 sec" |

### Parallel Agents

When several steps of one kind have no target, like agents, the group counts them: "Running 3 agents" with "1 of 3", then "Ran 3 agents". Agents that finish together are announced together, like "3 agents finished, 1 failed".

### Opening and Closing

A group opens while it runs, and by itself whenever a step waits for a person, even after someone closed it, so it never hides an approval. About a second after it finishes it folds, once, unless someone opened or closed it, and never while focus or a text selection is inside it. Find in page opens it too. Pass `open` with `onOpenChange` to control it.

With fewer than `minItems` items (3 by default), there's no group line: the rows show flat, since grouping two rows hides more than it saves.

### Motion

The group opens and closes like a [Step](./step.mdx), and a row that arrives while it's open rises in once; nothing plays when it opens. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, or with `motion="reduced"`, the content fades over 120 ms instead, and with `motion="none"` everything changes at once.
