Use a notice for something worth reading that isn't a message, like a rate limit, a lost connection or a finished setup. Its mark and colors say whether it's information, a success, a warning or an error.

## Composition

```text
Notice
├── NoticeIcon
├── NoticeTitle
├── NoticeDescription
├── NoticeActions
└── NoticeDismiss
```

`Notice` renders these parts: a note named by its `label`. `NoticeIcon` is the status mark, named **Info**, **Success**, **Warning** or **Error**, so the variant has words as well as a color. `NoticeTitle` shows the `label`, `NoticeDescription` holds the notice's `children`, `NoticeActions` holds its `actions`, and `NoticeDismiss` is the **Dismiss** button. To arrange the parts yourself, render them inside `NoticeRoot`.

## Usage

### Variants

Pass `variant` as `"info"` (the default), `"success"`, `"warning"` or `"error"`. Each has its own mark and tint, which stay readable in light and dark, and the mark keeps its glyph in forced colors. Put buttons that act on the notice, like **Retry**, in `actions`.

```tsx
<Notice variant="error" label="Couldn't reach the model" actions={<button type="button">Retry</button>}>
  Check your connection and try again.
</Notice>
```

### Dismiss

Pass `onDismiss` to show **Dismiss**. Pressing it moves focus out of the notice first, to the element `focusFallback` returns, else to the next element Tab reaches after the notice (the last one before it when nothing follows). Then the notice fades out, and `onDismiss` is called: remove the notice then. Return a promise to keep it until the promise settles; a rejection brings the notice back.

```tsx
const [shown, setShown] = useState(true);

{shown ? (
  <Notice variant="warning" label="Rate limited" onDismiss={() => setShown(false)}>
    Responses are slower for the next few minutes.
  </Notice>
) : null}
```

A notice you remove without **Dismiss** leaves at once, like any other content you stop rendering.

### Announcements

A notice that's on the page as it loads is read with the page and isn't announced. One you add later rises in and its `label` is announced, once, as is a change of `variant`. Editing its text isn't announced.

### Motion

A notice added later rises and fades in, and **Dismiss** fades it out. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, or with `motion="reduced"`, it only fades, and with `motion="none"` nothing animates.

### Agent

An agent connected with `createAgent` finds the notice by its label. `dismiss()` does what **Dismiss** does, offered while `onDismiss` is set and the notice isn't already leaving. It doesn't ask a person first.
